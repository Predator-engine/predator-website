About Predator

Predator is a UCI-compatible chess engine focused on dynamic, sharp play and modern search techniques. Built for practical strength, it combines speed, clarity of options and a distinctive identity.

Key Features

  • UCI engine with MultiPV, Syzygy tablebases and flexible options.
  • Support for modern hardware (AVX2 builds, multi-threading, large hash).
  • Configurable opening books (FenBook, CTG, multiple sources).
  • Human-like randomness and strength scaling for training and fun.
